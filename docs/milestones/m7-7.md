# Milestone 7.7 — Welcome & stats

Read CLAUDE.md, docs/brief.md and docs/roadmap.md first, then plan the following. Work on a branch (`feat/m7-7-welcome-stats`), not `main`.

## Goal

A welcoming start screen that shows what the profile has been up to and gets you back to practising in one key press, and a stats page that puts everything in one explorable place. References (exact sizes, spacing, colours): `docs/design/m7-7/source/QGWelcomeA.dc.html` and `QGStats.dc.html`, built with the milestone 6 components and tokens. Sample numbers in the mockups are placeholders.

## Decisions

### Navigation

1. **Welcome** lives at `#/welcome`; the player stays at `#/`. A new Appearance (or General) setting **"Start on"**: Welcome (default) / Player. With Player, the app opens on the player with the last entry loaded, as today.
2. **Home icon** at the top of the player's left menu (same dim/hover style), shortcut **`H`**; also a "Home" button in the Library / Bookmarks / Stats page headers.
3. **Stats icon** in the left menu (as in the mockup), shortcut **`S`**; also "See all stats" links on Welcome's tiles and in Settings → Profiles. Stats lives at `#/stats`.
4. Both new commands go into the command registry (rebindable, listed in the cheat sheet). Check the defaults don't conflict.

### Welcome (mockup A)

5. **Greeting** by local time of day — "Good morning" (5–12), "Good afternoon" (12–18), "Good evening" (18–23), "Good night" (23–5) — with the profile name in the secondary colour, and the localised date above it. A profile still named "Me" gets "Welcome back".
6. **Pick up where you left off:** the profile's last loaded entry (bookmark or Full song) with song, composer, the bookmark line (icon + name in secondary), its range bar with a marker where playback last stopped, and "Last played ⟨yesterday at 21:40⟩ · stopped at bar 26 · ⟨plays⟩ · ⟨time⟩".
   - Store the last stopped position (bar) per profile with `lastOpened` whenever playback pauses/stops or the page is left.
   - **Resume** (primary button, `Enter`) loads that entry, seeks to the stored bar (inside the loop; otherwise the loop start per the loop-bounds rule) and opens the player paused there, ready to play. **Choose another…** opens the song picker.
   - No history yet: the card offers the first bundled song ("Start with Minuet in G").
7. **This week:** minutes this week (locale week start) vs the same days last week ("+25% vs last week", hidden when last week had nothing), with a 7-day bar chart (today in primary, other days secondary, empty days a faint stub).
8. **Last 12 weeks:** calendar heatmap (single hue, secondary, 5 steps incl. empty; today outlined/primary) with a Less–More legend, and the **current streak** ("4 days in a row"; counts while today or yesterday was practised; hidden at 0).
9. **Rotating highlight:** one at a time, cross-fading every 8 s, paused while hovered or focused; dots show and select the position; under reduced motion it still fades but doesn't move. Candidates (only those with data, at most 5, most interesting first):
   - tempo progress: a bookmark range whose highest tempo rose by ≥ 5 points in the last 30 days ("B section went from 70% to 85% this month", with a small trend line);
   - favourite song this month (by time);
   - most practised bookmark this week;
   - longest session this month;
   - milestones (total time passes 10 h / 25 h / 50 h / 100 h; 100 / 500 / 1000 plays).
10. **Maybe next:** up to two suggestion chips — the most practised bookmark not played for ≥ 7 days, and a song never played. Clicking loads it and opens the player.
11. **Profile chip** top right (colour badge + name): opens the profile switcher with 2+ profiles, otherwise Settings → Profiles.
12. **Backdrop:** the player scene with the resume entry loaded (paused), blurred and dimmed behind Welcome, so Resume is instant and the piano samples are already loading. If that isn't practical, the library's last-frame snapshot, then the static gradient. State the choice in the plan.
13. The left menu shows on Welcome too (Home lit).

### Stats page (mockup)

14. **Header:** Back/Home, title "Stats · ⟨profile⟩", range **Week / Month / Year / All**, a **song filter** (All songs, or one song), **Export CSV**.
15. **Six headline numbers** for the range: practice time, sessions, average session, days practised, plays, longest streak — each with the change vs the previous range of the same length where it makes sense.
16. **Minutes per day** chart: per day for Week and Month, per week for Year, per month for All; today/current period in primary, others secondary; recessive grid, hover tooltip per bar.
17. **Top songs** by time (horizontal bars, single hue). With a song selected: **top bookmarks** of that song plus Full song and "Other setups".
18. **Calendar heatmap**: last 6 months (Year/All: 12 months), same steps as Welcome.
19. **When you practise**: weekday × time-of-day grid (morning / midday / afternoon / evening / night), single hue.
20. **Tempo progress** for a chosen bookmark (picker in the card header): per week, the highest tempo % played on that bookmark's bar range (any setup with that range), as a 2 px line with ≥ 8 px points and the latest value labelled. With fewer than 2 data points, a short empty state.
21. Every chart has a hover tooltip and a "Show as table" toggle (accessibility); no chart relies on colour alone. Colours: secondary as the single data hue, primary only for "today / current".
22. **Export CSV:** the play records for the current range and filter, one row per record (date, time, song, bookmark name or "Full song" / "Other setup", minutes, plays, tempo %, loop, hands, pre-roll, metronome), UTF-8 with a header row, file name `pianomeister-⟨profile⟩-⟨range⟩.csv`.
23. All stats are computed from the play log (brief), per active profile, in local calendar days (reuse `dailyMinutes`).

### Agreed when planning

24. **Backdrop:** Welcome is a child route of the player, over the live paused scene (the samples load while you read; Resume is instant). Stats uses the last-frame snapshot, like the library.
25. **Ranges are rolling windows**, labelled honestly: "7 days · 30 days · 12 months · All"; deltas read "vs previous 30 days" etc. Welcome's "This week" stays the calendar week (locale start).
26. **Headers:** "‹ Player" stays, with a **Home** button beside it on Library, Bookmarks and Stats.
27. **H and S** are in the Everywhere scope; Enter on Welcome resumes through "Open the selection"; Resume has the focus on arrival.
28. **Calendar shades:** fixed thresholds — none / 1–14 / 15–29 / 30–44 / 45+ minutes.
29. **Start on** applies when the app is opened at the site root (no hash); an explicit `#/` link and a reload of the player stay on the player.

## Tests

- **Unit:** greeting by hour; streak (today, yesterday, gaps, DST); week comparison; highlight selection and ordering; tempo progress series; suggestion picking; range bucketing (day/week/month); headline numbers and deltas; CSV rows (escaping, time zone).
- **E2e:** start setting (Welcome vs Player); Home icon and `H`; Stats icon and `S`; Resume returns to the stored bar of the last entry; Choose another opens the picker; highlights rotate and pause on hover (fake clock); suggestion chip loads; stats range and song filter change the numbers; Show as table; CSV download content; empty states with no history.
- **Visual:** Welcome (with history, and empty), Stats (Month all songs, Year with a song selected), 1280×720 and 1024×768, Glacier and Ember. Check them against the sources yourself.
