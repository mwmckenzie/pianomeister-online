# Milestone 9.5 — Playlist mode

Added 2026-11-23. Builds on 9, 9.1 (looks) and 9.2 (sessions, tracking-suspended switch). Branch: `feat/m9-5-playlists`, from `main`.

Play full songs back to back as background music, with the piano and the highway as a visualizer. Listening is not practice: nothing is logged.

Design boards (canvas section "11 · Playlist mode (9.5)"; sources in `docs/design/m9-5/source/`):

- `QGPlaylists`: the Playlists tab;
- `QGListen`: listening mode, HUD Show / Minimal / Hidden;
- `QGCrossfade`: the continuous highway with the seam;
- `QGWelcomePlaylists`: the Welcome tile.

Every new setting follows the usual rules: a reset icon, a disabled hint where a rule applies, clamping on load and import, and unit tests. Listening settings are app settings, not look keys, unless stated.

## A. Data

1. **Playlist** (per profile, in Dexie, with a version bump):
   - `id` (UUID), `profileId`, `name`;
   - `lookId`: a look id, or `null` = "Current look (no change)";
   - `entries`: `{ songId, lookId | null }[]`, in order; the same song may appear more than once;
   - `shuffle` (bool), `repeat` (`playlist` / `one` / `off`);
   - `createdAt`, `updatedAt`.
2. **Missing references never break a playlist:**
   - A deleted song: its entry stays, shown as "Missing song", and playback skips it.
   - A deleted look: the playlist or the entry falls back to "Current look", shown with a small hint.
3. **Backups** include playlists, with the same collision rules as looks: identical skipped, same id with different content added under a new id, a name clash gets "(2)", nothing local ever overwritten.
4. **Export and import:** `.pmplaylist.json` (`{ format: "pianomeister-playlist", version, playlists: [...] }`).
   - One playlist from its "Export" button; "Export all" from the ⋯ menu.
   - Songs are matched on import by the library's song identity (the content hash or whatever identity the backup already uses). Songs not found become "Missing song" entries, listed in the import toast, e.g. "Imported 'Party visualizer' · 1 song not in your library".
   - Looks referenced by id: a built-in id always resolves. A saved look missing locally falls back to "Current look". The file may carry the look itself (as in a look file), so offer "Import its looks too" when it does.
   - Import never starts playback.

## B. Playlists tab (board `QGPlaylists`)

1. A third tab next to Library | Bookmarks, with the same page layout.
2. **Left column:** the profile's playlists (name, "N songs · total length", look swatch and name). "New playlist" and "Import" in the header.
3. **Editor:**
   - Name (inline edit).
   - Play, Shuffle (toggle), Repeat (Playlist / One song / Off), Look (select: Current look, built-ins, saved looks), Export, and ⋯ (Rename, Duplicate, Export all, Delete with Undo).
   - The header shows "N songs · length · crossfade X s".
4. **Song rows:** drag handle, #, title / composer, length, a **per-song look picker**, and remove (with Undo).
   - The picker shows "Playlist look · ⟨name⟩" (dim) until overridden; an override shows the look's name.
   - Drag to reorder, with keyboard alternatives (Alt+↑/↓ on a focused row) and a screen-reader announcement.
5. **Add songs panel:** search the library; "+" adds a song; "✓" marks songs already in the playlist (clicking ✓ adds it again, with a toast "Added again · Undo").
6. **Add to playlist…** in the Library's song actions and in the song picker: a menu listing playlists plus "New playlist…".
7. **Empty states:** no playlists ("Make a playlist to listen to songs back to back" + New playlist); an empty playlist ("Add songs from the panel on the right").
8. List keys work as in Library and Bookmarks (roles: playlists = side list, songs = main list).

## C. Listening mode (board `QGListen`)

1. **Starting:** Play on a playlist, Play on a Welcome tile, or Play from a song row (starts at that song). The player opens in listening mode.
2. **While listening:**
   - **Tracking suspended** is on (from 9.2): no plays, practice time, sessions or listening stats, and the session/idle timer is paused, so Home never auto-opens.
   - **Full songs at 100 %:** no loops, pre-roll or count-in, and no bookmark is loaded. Tempo can be changed for the listening session only. It resets when listening ends and never touches the songs' saved settings.
3. **The look overlay:**
   - The playlist's look, or a song's override, is applied with the **pure temporary-look overlay** (9.1): the user's own settings are never written.
   - Leaving listening restores exactly what was there.
   - Playfield sidebar edits during listening change the overlay only. The look picker offers "Save as new look…", plus "Update playlist look" if it came from the playlist. A small line in the sidebar says "Changes here last until you leave listening".
4. **HUD:** setting "Listening HUD": Show (default) / Minimal / Hidden, in Settings → Appearance, plus a command to cycle it.
   - **Show:** the normal HUD, plus "⟨playlist⟩ · song N of M" above the title and "Up next · ⟨title · composer⟩" under the position. The right column holds previous / play-pause / next **song**, shuffle and repeat (with their state labels), and time. There's no loop or tempo row except a small tempo % when it isn't 100.
   - **Minimal:** only the title and composer, top left.
   - **Hidden:** no HUD at all, a pure visualizer. The timeline is hidden too.
   - **In Minimal and Hidden,** a small previous / pause / next sits bottom right. It appears and hides with the menu icons (it follows the idle-hide setting).
5. **The menu column is always reachable** in every mode and follows idle-hide. First item: **Leave listening (Esc)**, in the secondary colour. Then **Mute (M)**, a quick toggle with its own state colour. Then Playlists, Playfield and Settings. Opening the playfield sidebar, settings or the cheat sheet works as usual; Esc closes the open panel first.
6. **Song announcement:** when a new song starts (after the seam crosses the now line), in Show and Minimal the title and composer fade in centred ("Now playing · N of M"), hold about 3 s, and fade out. Not in Hidden. Reduced motion: no fade, just shown and removed.
7. **Leaving listening:** Esc (when no panel is open), the Leave button, or opening any song normally (picker, Library, a bookmark, Welcome Resume). It stops playback, restores the look, ends tracking-suspended and re-arms the session timer, and returns to the normal player with the last song still loaded (not playing). Closing or reloading the app also ends listening; a reload never resumes listening mode.
8. **Commands** (in `COMMANDS.ts`, with layout-aware bindings, scope "Listening", shown in the cheat sheet):
   - Next song, Previous song, Play/pause, Mute, Cycle listening HUD, Leave listening.
   - Propose bindings that don't clash; reuse the player's play/pause key.
   - **Previous song** restarts the current song if more than 3 s in, otherwise goes to the previous song.
9. **Shuffle:** a shuffled order with no repeats until every entry has played, then reshuffled (with repeat playlist on). The current song stays first when shuffle is switched on mid-playlist.
10. **Repeat:**
    - Playlist: wraps to the start, with a crossfade.
    - One song: the song crossfades into itself.
    - Off: stops after the last song and stays in listening mode, paused, showing "Playlist finished · Play again".

## D. Continuous highway and crossfade (board `QGCrossfade`)

1. **Setting "Crossfade between songs":** 0–8 s, step 0.5, default 2 s (Settings → Audio, or wherever playback settings live).
   - At 0: no overlap, and about 1 s of empty highway (silence) before the next song.
2. **Audio:** equal-power crossfade. The next song starts `crossfade` seconds before the current one ends; the ending song's gain fades out over that time and the next song's fades in. "End" means the last note's release; trailing silence in a file is trimmed for this purpose.
3. **The highway is continuous:**
   - The next song's notes follow the current song's on the same highway, placed in **time**, so tempo and meter changes between songs join cleanly.
   - During the overlap, both songs' notes pass the now line together.
   - Bar and beat lines belong to whichever song owns that part of the highway, and switch at the seam.
4. **Seam line:** a line across the highway where the next song starts, in the secondary colour with glow (reusing the 9.2 loop-line renderer), with the next song's "Title · Composer" written along it near the left edge.
   - It shows in every HUD mode, Hidden included.
   - Settings: on/off (default on), plus the colour from the loop lines' palette (default Secondary). These two are look keys.
5. **Crossfade band:** the part of the highway where both songs sound gets a faint tint. The ending song's notes dim with its audio gain; the next song's notes are at full strength.
6. **Per-song looks across the seam:** when the next song has a different look, its look takes over as the seam crosses the now line.
   - Colours and numeric settings ease over max(crossfade, 0.5 s); discrete settings (fills, styles, toggles) switch at the midpoint.
   - Camera and look-ahead ease too.
   - No easing under reduced motion.
   - The plan should say what's feasible; if easing everything is costly, propose a reduced set.
7. **Loading:** the next song's MIDI is parsed and its samples are ready well before the seam (start at least crossfade + 5 s ahead). If it isn't ready in time, delay the seam rather than glitch, and log it in debug stats.
8. **Effects** (particles, pulses) work for both songs during the overlap.
9. **Performance:** report draw calls during an overlap, against a single song, at Off and Medium.

## E. Welcome tile (board `QGWelcomePlaylists`)

1. A "Listen" card in the Welcome right column, under the 12-week calendar: up to 2–3 playlists (the most recently played first, then the newest), each with a play button, plus "All playlists" linking to the tab.
2. The card is hidden when the profile has no playlists, or shows a quiet "Make a playlist" link (decide in the plan; prefer hidden).
3. "Recently played" for playlists is the one thing listening remembers: a `lastPlayedAt` on the playlist only. No stats.

## F. Not in this milestone

- Bookmarks having a look (backlog; it reuses the same overlay function, so keep that function pure).
- Practice-mode playlists (bookmarks in playlists, repeats per entry). Playlists stay full songs only.
