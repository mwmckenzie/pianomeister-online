# Milestone 8 — Section overview & loop editor

Read CLAUDE.md, docs/brief.md and docs/roadmap.md first, then plan the following. Work on a branch (`feat/m8-overview`), not `main`.

## Goal

See the whole song at a glance and work with bars visually: an **overview strip** inside the player for quick loop and bookmark work while practising, and a full **loop editor** page for precise editing. References (exact sizes, spacing, colours): `docs/design/m8/source/QGOverviewA.dc.html` (strip) and `QGOverviewB.dc.html` (editor). The notes in the mockups are placeholders.

## Decisions

### Shared

1. **Piano-roll drawing:** notes in their pitch-class colours (brief palette), right hand at full strength, left hand at ~50 %; hidden channels aren't drawn. The x-axis is **musical time** (ticks), so bars of different meters get their true widths; bar lines with every 4th bar stronger and numbered; beat lines faint in the editor. Pitch runs low at the bottom, high at the top, fitted to the song's pitch range (stored facts) with a small margin.
2. **Rendering:** a 2D canvas (not SVG — songs can have thousands of notes), redrawn on zoom/scroll/resize/theme/hand-assignment changes only; the playhead, loop band, selection and lanes are a separate light layer. Nothing is drawn while the strip is closed. Device-pixel-ratio aware.
3. **Loop band** in the secondary colour with drag handles at both ends (bar numbers shown while dragging). Handle drags follow the existing loop rules: range kept valid (`adjustLoopRange`), applied on release with one jump to the loop start (brief 8), temporary vs global exactly as other loop changes (brief 69), now-playing line and attribution follow (7.6).
4. **Drag across the notes** makes a **selection** (primary, dashed outline; snaps to whole bars). Releasing shows the **action bar** next to it: "Bars 41–48 · 8 bars", **Loop these bars** (primary), **Save as bookmark** (one click, auto name, no exact duplicates — same as the player's save), and × to clear. Esc clears. Nothing changes until an action is chosen.
5. **Click (no drag) seeks** to the start of the clicked bar, with the loop-bounds rule (inside an active loop only).
6. **Playhead** shown live (white, as on the timeline). When zoomed in, the view follows the playhead while playing ("Follow" on by default; manual scrolling turns it off until the next play or a click on "Follow").
7. **Keyboard:** the roll is focusable; ←/→ move a bar cursor, Shift+←/→ extend a selection, Enter = Loop these bars, B = Save as bookmark (existing command), Esc clears. Screen-reader labels state the bar range.
8. **Zoom:** Ctrl/⌘ + wheel and pinch zoom around the pointer; plain wheel / two-finger scroll pans horizontally.

### Overview strip (in the player, mockup A)

9. Opens from the top timeline (a small expand chevron at its right end) or **`V`** (new Player command, rebindable). The open state persists (UI state). When open, the strip replaces the thin timeline and the HUD columns move down below it; nothing covers the highway's lower part or the keyboard.
10. Header: "Overview", song name and bar count, the right/left hand legend, a zoom control (Whole song … 4 bars), **Open editor**, and the close chevron.
11. **Bookmark lane** above the notes: the song's bookmarks as labelled ranges (stacked rows when they overlap, at most 2 rows in the strip, "+N" when more), the loaded/matching one highlighted (7.6). Clicking a range **loads** that bookmark (a normal load). The strip never edits bookmarks.
12. Auto-hide: the strip's controls fade with the other player controls (brief 64) but the strip itself (notes, loop, playhead) stays visible, like the timeline.

### Loop editor page (mockup B)

13. Route `#/editor` as a **child route of the player** (like Welcome): the player stays mounted with the scene frozen/hidden behind the page, so **playback keeps running** while you edit (Space and the transport keys work; the roll's playhead follows). Opened from the strip's "Open editor", the bookmark manager ("Open in editor" for the selected bookmark), and a new command **"Open loop editor"** [`E`]. "‹ Player" returns.
14. Header: "‹ Player", title "⟨Song⟩ · Overview", zoom presets (Whole song / 32 / 16 / 8 bars) plus continuous zoom, right/left hand visibility toggles (view only), and "Play from bar N" (the selection's or loop's start).
15. Pitch labels (C notes) on the left, beat grid, loop band with labelled handles, playhead.
16. **Bookmark lanes** under the notes, stacked when ranges overlap, all of the song's bookmarks. Clicking selects one (side panel). **This page is a second bookmark editor** (alongside the manager): dragging a selected bookmark's ends or its body edits its range, with the same rules as the manager — saved on release, auto name follows, Undo toast, "Same settings as …" when it matches another bookmark, ranges kept inside the song. Brief decision 69 changes from "only in the bookmark manager" to "only in the bookmark manager and the loop editor; never from the player screen".
17. **Minimap** at the bottom: the whole song with the current view as a draggable window and the loop range marked.
18. **Side panel** (selected bookmark): name, summary, From/To fields (edit the range), stats (Played, Practice, Last), **Load in player**, **Edit in bookmark manager**. With no bookmark selected: the current selection's or loop's details and the two actions from the action bar.

### Agreed when planning

19. **Strip placement:** the HUD columns stay exactly as they are and the thin timeline stays at the top. The strip sits directly under the timeline, between the left and right columns (their measured widths, ~24 px gaps, recomputed on resize and when the playfield panel opens or closes). While it is open the title, composer and now-playing line truncate at its left edge (tooltip as now). At narrow widths the strip shrinks; zoom and the editor cover detail.
20. **Saving a selection** doesn't load it: toast "Saved "⟨name⟩"" with **Load**, **Rename** and **Undo**.
21. **Play from bar N** shows where play will really start (the loop-bounds rule); fallback: the selection's start, else the loop start, else the bar cursor.
22. **Editing the loaded bookmark's range** in the editor moves the loop with one jump, unless a temporary loop change is active (it wins).
23. **Also agreed:** an **Open editor** button in the strip header (missing from mockup A); presets in the order Whole · 32 · 16 · 8 bars; the Follow chip shows only while following is off; the pitch range comes from the loaded song's hand-assigned notes; no note hit-testing (no interaction needs it); `B` with a selection saves the selection; the focused overview keeps its arrow keys; editor range edits get an Undo toast (the manager keeps none); a generated ~340-bar fixture for perf; the strip's lane has at most 2 rows (the notes area gives up 26 px for the second).
24. **Round 1 review:** the strip hides below ~360 px between the columns (the chevron and `V` then open the loop editor); bookmark lanes are one Tab stop with arrow keys (roving tabindex); the editor's hand toggles are remembered (UI state); a **Loop editor** item in the left menu after Change song (its own icon, `E`, lit while the editor is open), the menu items 36 px apart so the column still clears the keyboard.
25. **Round 2 review:** the loop editor is a full page (no app menu): **Home** beside "‹ Player" in its header. The left menu is at its height limit at 1280×720 with two profiles; a further item needs the menu to move up or reflow.

## Core and engine

- `core/overview/`: bar layout in ticks (from the bar map), visible-range math, zoom/pan clamping, hit-testing (pointer → bar / note / lane item), lane stacking for overlapping ranges, selection snapping — all pure and unit-tested.
- A shared `PianoRollCanvas` component (strip and editor) with the layer split from decision 2.
- No changes to the 3D engine except reading the song model it already has.

## Tests

- **Unit:** bar layout with meter changes and a pickup bar; zoom/pan clamping; hit-testing at edges; lane stacking; selection snapping; keyboard selection logic.
- **E2e:** V opens/closes the strip and it persists; click seeks (and respects an active loop); drag selection → Loop these bars sets the loop and jumps once; Save as bookmark from a selection (and the "already saved" case); loop handle drag; lane click loads a bookmark; Open editor keeps playback running; editor: drag a bookmark's end → saved, auto name updates, Undo restores; minimap drag pans; keyboard selection + Enter; zoom presets.
- **Perf:** strip open while playing the longest song you have (e.g. 340 bars): frame time unchanged within noise; strip redraw on zoom under ~16 ms.
- **Visual:** strip (whole song, zoomed, with a selection and action bar), editor (32 bars, bookmark selected), 1280×720 and 1024×768, Glacier and Ember.
